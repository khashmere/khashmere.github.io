CASE STUDY · MSP PARTNERSHIP

One scheduler. Two systems. One fix that became a revenue line.

Every MSP wants AI as a new revenue line. But AI is useless without clean data underneath — so here, the data work came first, and the AI followed.

Challenge

Every event, typed twice.

A scheduler built each event roster by hand in payroll, then typed it again into a separate event system. Slow — and the two drifted, so shifts went uncovered or people got paid for shifts they never worked. Leadership knew AI could help, but asked the right question first: out of a dozen ideas, which one do we do first?

Solution

Start where the pain already is.

Our forward-deployed engineers sequenced ten priorities, configured Microsoft Fabric, stood up governance, and wired semantic models into Copilot — so answers now come in the flow of work. Then we automated the payroll–event interface. Entered once, correct everywhere.
